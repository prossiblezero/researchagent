# H1 Dataset Rights

The H1 dev and test datasets are self-authored for this repository. The
project contributors dedicate the original dataset fields they authored,
including questions, reference answers, acceptable variants, evidence
requirements, refusal rules, labels, and metadata, under CC0 1.0 to the extent
they hold those rights:

https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The cited external documents are references only. Their text, titles,
trademarks, and other third-party material are not placed under CC0 by this
dataset and remain subject to their respective owners' terms. Per-case
`provenance.source_url` and `provenance.sources` entries identify those
references for audit; they do not copy or relicense the referenced works.
